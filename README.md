A dark editorial cover that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one 1024x1024 image: Xi, Putin and Trump converging on Shenzhen for a first three-way summit, US strikes on Iran's Qeshm Island and the Gulf response, the US losing its measles-free status, Alaska flipping the Senate to 56-44, and Voyager 1 crossing one light-day from Earth. The picture is drawn deterministically with Pillow by `render.py` from the hand-picked stories in `news_data.md`; `main.py` (standard library only) copies the finished `news_image.png` to `/output/entry.png`.

## Run it
